- **Task**: Bug [P1]: Voiceover not playing in generated video

## Accomplishments
- **Fixed Silent Synthesis**: Identified that `AVAudioSession` was changed to `.playAndRecord` in `VideoNarrationService` but never activated with `setActive(true)`. This caused `AVSpeechSynthesizer` to produce silent/empty buffers on some iOS versions or when the hardware mute switch was on.
- **Optimized Audio Session Churn**: Refactored `VideoNarrationService` to manage the audio session once per batch of narration clips, reducing the overhead of 24 session transitions per 12-segment video.
- **Enhanced Diagnostics**: Added `print` logging in `VideoAssemblyEngine` to track narration track addition, durations, and volumes during the composition phase.
- **Enabled Cosmos Voiceover**: Per the bug report's Acceptance Criteria, enabled voiceover narration for the Cosmos animated video (narrates title and date).
- **Silent File Detection**: Added warning logging in `VideoNarrationService` if speech synthesis produces zero audio frames, allowing for faster debugging of empty `.caf` files.
