- **PC-262 [P1]**: VideoAssemblyEngine — no music plays in generated video

## Analysis Summary
Investigated missing background music in generated videos.
- **Root Cause 1 (VideoAssemblyEngine)**: Raw AAC ADTS files are often problematic for AVURLAsset. The engine was failing to find tracks or duration, causing it to skip adding the music track to the composition.
- **Root Cause 2 (MusicLibrary)**: ODR resolution was brittle, and fallback to bundled tracks was failing if the tracks were only marked as ODR in the project setup.

**Fixes Applied**:
- **VideoAssemblyEngine.swift**: 
  - Switched to `loadTracks(withMediaType: .audio)` for more robust track discovery.
  - Enhanced duration resolution logic with better optional handling and `AVAudioFile` fallbacks.
  - Hardened the `insertMusic` loop to handle potential NaN or indefinite durations safely.
  - Aligned music track loading with the successful narration track pattern.
- **MusicLibrary.swift**:
  - Improved `resolveURL` to be more thorough in searching ODR and bundled locations.
  - Enhanced fallback mechanism to try style-specific candidates before universal fallbacks.
  - Added detailed logging to surface resolution failures.
