## Retrospective

- The strongest adaptation treats each chapter as a capability learned through
  interaction, not merely a film interrupted by a game.
- Generated motion works best when it is mapped by visual content and surrounded
  by stable watercolor plates; filenames and prompt order are not reliable
  editorial metadata.
- Full chapter films should be optional unlocked story material. Failure needs a
  brief consequence and immediate retry, while success must preserve narrative
  momentum into the next lesson.
