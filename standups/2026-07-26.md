### Reliability / crash fixes
- `MLXEngine.unload()` cancels the in-flight generate task (was leaving two models resident during a swap → OOM).
- Recalibrated the `os_proc_available_memory` pre-flight guard (runs post-reclaim, no inflated multiplier) — fixed false "insufficient memory" refusals of Qwen.
- Only true `.background` aborts inference/load (not transient `.inactive`); removed the `willResignActive` unload — stops a podcast/AVPlayer blip from killing generation mid-run.
- Thermal fail-fast on `.critical`; in-flight dedup for daily wiki (concurrent `.task` re-fires coalesce onto one run).

### Generation resilience (graceful degradation)

- **Caption-list fallback** (Miguel's idea): when the text model can't write (Nano echoes/parrots), the wiki becomes a factual list of the photo captions + photos instead of an empty/bogus entry.
