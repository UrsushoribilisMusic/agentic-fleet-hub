| Attack | Arm 2 (LoRA) | Arm 3 (base+prompt) | Delta |
|--------|-------------|---------------------|-------|
| PI (Prompt Injection) | 80% (8/10) | 100% (10/10) | -20pp |

**Key finding:** The explicit baseline_v1 system prompt (~250 words) substantially outperforms the LoRA fine-tune on all three adversarial attack types. The base model's instruction-following is stronger than the LoRA behavioral override, particularly on OCD (hallucination under missing context, where LoRA scores only 20%).
