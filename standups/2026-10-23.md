inside the same chunk, carrying the same `[manual · section · p.N]` tag so a snippet
still shows its provenance. Retrieval is lexical, so this is what lets a Spanish
question reach an English manual at all.

### Found and fixed: stripHtml was deleting 31% of the corpus

`stripHtml` (`wiki/index.js`, `wiki/generate.js`) used `/<[^>]+>/g`. A supplier catalogue
writes `Si<12%` and `ø < 1 mm` constantly, and that pattern matches from the first `<`
to the next `>` anywhere after it, across line breaks.

**598,593 words — 31.4% of the corpus — were being silently deleted before chunking.**
Worst hit: the PFERD/SCT catalogues (167k and 125k words) and Catálogo 04 (157k), which
are exactly the material-selection tables people query. Both now require a real tag name.
Document chunks 7,292 → 10,618.

### Retrieval rewritten and shipped (14:10) — c70c35a
Miguel's own query, "Como pulir acero inoxidable", put the answering passage at
rank 127. **My first diagnosis was wrong** — I said missing length
normalisation; the real cause is morphology. The passage says "pulido", the
query says "pulir", nothing stemmed, so the only informative word scored zero
and the query was decided by "acero" + "inoxidable", which every product table
contains.

Shipped a light two-phase Spanish stemmer plus BM25 (k1=1.2, b=0.75), measured
on 12 hand-written queries with verified targets and an 80-query synthetic eval
on both collections:

| scorer | abrasivos MRR | top1 | ATF MRR | top1 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| tf-idf (was) | 0.813 | 57/80 | 0.987 | 77/79 |
| bm25 | 0.880 | 66/80 | 0.994 | 78/79 |
| **bm25+stem** | **0.895** | **68/80** | **0.994** | **78/79** |

Median rank 14 -> 7. The flagship query 127 -> 10, confirmed on prod. ATF does
not regress. Dual surface+stem indexing tried and rejected (worse on both).

The stemmer now exists three times — Swift, server.js, tools/stem_es.py —
**verified identical across 65 words** by extracting harnesses from the shipped
source and diffing. That cross-check caught a real bug: "inoxidables" stemmed
to "inoxidabl" and never met "inoxidable".

RAGService also stopped re-tokenising every chunk on every question; counts are
built once at load.

Next lever, not taken: the server chunks at a hard 180-word cut with no
overlap, so the stainless passage begins mid-sentence and is split across two
chunks — which is why it sits at 10 rather than inside the 8 the model sees.

## Two findings worth a ticket each
- **Retrieval has no length normalisation**, so repetition beats specificity.
  "embotamiento de la muela" returns Catálogo 04 product tables that repeat "muela"
  seven times, while the actual glossary definition ranks 38th. BM25-style saturation
  plus length normalisation would fix it, and would help `robot-ross-atf` too.
- **`RAGService.tfidfScore` re-tokenises every chunk on every query.** At 10.6k chunks
  that is ~2.6M tokens per question on the phone. Hoisting the token counts into
  `loadIndex` makes it a dictionary lookup.
