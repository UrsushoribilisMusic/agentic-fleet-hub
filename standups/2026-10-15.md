## Retrospective

- The fastest path was not accepting every generated frame, but combining generated motion with deliberately held watercolor plates and inspecting transitions frame by frame.
- Visual plausibility mattered as much as style consistency: incorrect screen geometry and repeated camera moves immediately read as production errors.
- The human-plus-agent workflow was strongest when each pass had a narrow responsibility: narrative direction, asset generation, implementation, critical review, and final QA.
