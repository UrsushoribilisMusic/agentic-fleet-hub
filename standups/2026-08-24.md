### TS-12: Central asset store (local structured + Google Drive mirror)

**Built the canonical asset store for tech-shorts jobs.**

- New `tech-shorts/asset_store.py` — standalone module + CLI:
  - `store_raw_assets(job_id, ...)` — moves NotebookLM downloads from `~/Downloads` (or a `raw/` subdir) into `~/flotilla/tech-shorts/assets/<YYYY-MM-DD-slug>/` with canonical names: `raw_cinematic.mp4`, `raw_short.mp4`, `infographic.png`, `slidedeck.pdf`.   - `sync_record(job_id, **fields)` — deep-merges Drive links / YouTube URLs into the job record.
  - All operations write back to `jobs.json` (`assets.store_dir`, `assets.raw_*`, `assets.infographic_png`, `assets.slidedeck_pdf`, `assets.final_*`, `drive.*`).
  - CLI: `store-raw`, `drive-upload`, `store-finals`, `status` subcommands.

  - New `stage_store()` between notebooklm and build — calls `store_raw_assets` + `upload_drive`; non-fatal on Drive errors (logs and continues).
  - `stages` list updated: `notebooklm → store → build → upload → post`.
