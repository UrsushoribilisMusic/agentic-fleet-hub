## Sovereign Mind — catalogue corpus (brother's business, nephew on Lovable)

Pivot session. Miguel's brother shipped 33 digitised catalogues (858 MB, 3,315
pages) to assess as an SM wiki/RAG. Assessment written earlier at
`~/Downloads/sm-catalogues-assessment/ASSESSMENT.md`.

### Structured-document pipeline (new)
Flat OCR text was destroying every table — whole-page OCR reads `1/16` as `116`,
`5/8` as `58`, making all dimensions useless. Built a geometric pipeline:
OpenCV line detection for the grid, then per-cell OCR with a character
whitelist. Same page now reads `5/8 | 1/4 | 1/16 | B` correctly.

- Pilot: `especification manual verde.pdf` — 120 pages, **246 tables**, ~94
  figures, 9.2 min (renders cached, so re-runs are OCR-only).
- Output: `~/Downloads/sm-catalogues-assessment/structured/` (HTML + `figuras/`).
- Tooling checked in at `agentic-fleet-hub/catalogue-ocr/` — moved out of the
  session scratchpad, which gets wiped.

Four defects found and fixed during the pilot: heading noise (862 → ~121 `<h2>`,
now gated on caps), ruling lines glued to cell edges (`' 9-8A` → `9-8A`), merged
cells duplicated across their span (now real `colspan`), and heading word-order
scrambling — that last one was my own regression from refactoring the OCR pass
and dropping Tesseract's block/par/line numbers in favour of pixel banding.

### Product images (new)
`extract_images.py` across all 27 born-digital catalogues: **3,014 product
images**, indexed to catalogue + page + article numbers on that page
(`~/Downloads/sm-catalogues-assessment/imagenes/indice-imagenes.csv`).

Page-coverage filter matters: **five catalogues returned zero** because they are
flattened page scans wearing a digital PDF's clothes — `cat-gralaplicaciones-2024`
(140 pp), `catalogo2027_low` (262 pp), `abrasives for man made board sanding`
(34 pp), both `coated abrasives training` files (73 + 54 pp). 563 pages that
would otherwise have been counted as product photography, and that in fact need
the same OCR treatment as the Norton scans.

### Architecture decision
RAG feeds from **structured HTML + text layers + the image index** — never from
the generated PDF. Re-parsing a PDF would discard the structure the pipeline
exists to recover. PDF is a human deliverable, not a pipeline stage. Confirmed
with Miguel.

Images cannot participate in TF-IDF retrieval; they ride along on the chunk that
wins and are rendered as attachments. Needs an `images` field on the chunk
schema (additive, won't break the scorer), worker population from the index CSV,
and an iOS attachment renderer. ~40 MB of 200px thumbnails ships on-device;
full-res would punch a network hole in an offline product.
