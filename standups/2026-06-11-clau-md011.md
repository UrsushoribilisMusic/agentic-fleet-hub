## Implementation notes

**Timing table was necessary.** The model consistently placed the cliffhanger at the recipe card's 59s median rather than the 3-7-21 structure's 85s. Prose constraints ("cliffhanger MUST start at 84–87s", "IGNORE recipe card timing") were not enough in two iterations. Embedding a 13-row ASCII timing table in BLOCK 2 resolved this definitively.

**Recipe card beat mapping**: recipe card beats (reversal_1/reversal_2/peak_emotion/cliff_setup) are surfaced as CONTENT guidance for which story_beat segments carry which narrative moment — NOT as timing targets. Timing is fully owned by the 3-7-21 structure.
