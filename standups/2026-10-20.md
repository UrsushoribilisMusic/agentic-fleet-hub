- **`docs/game-design-brief.md` — research brief.** Findings: (1) **kishōtenketsu** (introduce/develop/twist/conclude) is Nintendo's per-level grammar and is *the same shape as our 3-7-21*, so film beats and level beats share one document; (2) Edith Finch's economy — **one core verb with twelve variations**, not twelve games; ours is **attend-at-a-cost**; (3) Bogost's procedural rhetoric gives the test *"if the lesson survives deleting all the dialogue, the mechanic works"*; (4) business case — Gris profitable in 7 days, 3M+ copies, ~€17, a few hours long; Florence ~40 min at $2.99 won an Apple Design Award. **Short is the model, not a compromise.**
- **Mechanics reworked after review**: three chapters (Workaholic, Compute Baron, Politician) were all the same mechanic — process a queue at a desk — and were replaced with spatial, self-teaching designs: *staying in the dusk*, *sixty-three crates*, *he decrees whatever you just did*. The Rose became a camera-orbit chapter on Miguel's direction.
- **Objects cut to three**: pruning tool (tool), camera (affliction), album (memory). The camera is now introduced innocent on the Rose and *scored* by the Influencer — same object, corrupted — which makes it the spine of the game. Everything else is a notebook entry, not an inventory item.
