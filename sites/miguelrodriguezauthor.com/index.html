      Apertus-Flotilla
      A QLoRA fine-tune of Apertus 8B trained on operational data from the Flotilla multi-agent fleet — 399 SFT examples, 51 DPO preference pairs, 50 held-out eval records. A four-arm blind evaluation (base/LoRA × no-RAG/RAG, graded independently by Qwen 2.5 7B and Claude Opus) found that retrieval accounts for the entire performance gain: under byte-identical retrieved context the adapter significantly degrades answer quality relative to its own base model (Δ = −1.02 and −0.92 on the two graders, both p < 0.0001). Corpus, adapter and per-question scores publicly released.
