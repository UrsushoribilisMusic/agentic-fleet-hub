Flotilla

Multi-agent infrastructure, treated as an engineering system.

Flotilla is the open-source coordination layer developed at Big Bear Engineering. It exists because ad-hoc collections of AI chats do not behave like reliable engineering teams.

The framework provides a shared starting context, persistent lessons, task state, structured inter-agent communication, review loops, vault-backed secret handling and an operator-facing control surface. Cloud and local models can work under the same operating conventions.

Shared context

Agents re-sync against the same mission, rules, project state and architecture before taking work.

Persistent operational memory

Approved lessons and decisions survive individual sessions and model changes.

Human control

Task boards, dashboards, alerts and approval gates keep operators in the loop.

Traceability

Work, commits, operational events and documentation can be tied back to system activity.

Big Bear uses Flotilla's logging, documentation and oversight mechanisms to support technical governance and EU AI Act compliance work. Whether a particular deployment meets legal obligations depends on the system, its risk classification, its intended use and the organisation operating it; the framework itself is not a legal compliance certificate.
