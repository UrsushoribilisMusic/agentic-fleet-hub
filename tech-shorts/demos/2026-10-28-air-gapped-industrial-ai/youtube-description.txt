Life is about stories, and we have always used stories to transmit knowledge.

Sovereign Mind puts that knowledge in the hands of industrial technicians—even where cloud AI cannot go.

This demo shows open-weight AI running on an iPhone with local knowledge packs, source-backed answers, model and persona choices, multilingual conversations, and camera-based text recognition (OCR).

Technical manuals, operating procedures, and expertise become an editable Wiki and a local knowledge base. User feedback and model disposition signals help identify interactions worth reviewing. Proposed improvements are reviewed, edited, and approved by a human before updated knowledge reaches technicians.

Local inference means no per-query cloud inference fees and no need to send technicians' questions and proprietary documents to an external AI service.

We have an active test system with an industrial abrasives distributor and are in discussions with equipment manufacturers in Switzerland and Germany. The goal: shorter setup times, fewer unnecessary repairs, and fewer repeat visits—reducing service costs and improving our customers' bottom line.
