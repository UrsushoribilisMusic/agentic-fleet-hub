Short Version

Use Canis as an experimental AI tool. Verify important answers, especially in medical, legal, financial, safety, or other high-stakes contexts. The disposition readout is an interpretability signal, not a guarantee that an answer is correct.

Scope

Canis is designed to answer questions locally, use user-provided Sources, and show a model disposition readout. The app may be distributed through TestFlight before App Store release. Beta features can change, break, or be removed.

AI Outputs

  • Answers are generated by local language models and may be incomplete, outdated, or wrong.
  • Disposition labels such as confident, unsure, curious, concerned, reluctant, or warm describe model-behavior signals. They do not mean the model has emotions or human intent.
  • You are responsible for deciding whether an answer is appropriate for your use case.

Sources and User Documents

If you add documents as Sources, you confirm that you have the right to use those files in the app. Canis processes Sources locally for retrieval and citation, but you should not rely on it as the only copy or archive of important documents.
