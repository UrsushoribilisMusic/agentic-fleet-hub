Help with Canis.

Canis is an on-device AI assistant that answers questions, can use your local Sources, and shows a disposition readout while the model responds.

Common Questions

  • Do I need an account? No. Canis runs without a login.
  • Why does the model download take time? Canis runs the LLM locally, so the model has to be downloaded to the device before use. Wi-Fi is recommended.
  • What are Sources? Sources are documents you add so Canis can answer with local retrieval and citations. The app chunks and indexes those documents on the device.
  • Does the dog have feelings? No. The dog is a readable instrument panel for model behavior. Dispositions such as confident, unsure, curious, concerned, reluctant, or warm describe signals in the answer, not emotions.
  • What should I do if an answer looks wrong? Treat Canis as an experimental local AI tool. Check cited Sources, use the thumbs feedback controls, and send a support note if you can reproduce the issue.
