## Goal

Create a prospect-intelligence campaign for Sovereign Mind focused on DACH machine builders. The campaign should produce a ranked target list and a reusable dossier schema that Miguel and Reto can use to get real customer conversations.

## Why Now

Sovereign Mind has a concrete industrial use case: offline, source-grounded knowledge access for field and industrial teams. DACH machine builders are a strong early market to investigate because they sell complex equipment, maintain installed bases, support customers across borders, and often operate under strict trust, service, and data-governance expectations.

## Constraints

- Lead with customer pain, not technology.
- Use public sources only.
- Preserve source URLs for every factual claim.
- Do not use speculative claims in public-facing copy.
- Separate sourced facts, internal hypotheses, and open questions.
- Do not create execution tickets until Miguel approves the decision brief.

## Success Criteria

- A ranked prospect-list plan exists, including target segments, scoring criteria, and quality gates.
- A reusable dossier schema exists for target-account research.
- The plan identifies public source types and claim-safety rules.
- The plan gives Miguel and Reto a realistic path to exploratory conversations, not just a spreadsheet.
- The decision brief preserves agreement, disagreement, risks, open questions, and a ticket plan.

## Proposed Council Roster

- GTM planner: segment focus, account prioritization, and outreach angle.
- Research operations architect: source ledger, workflow, scoring, and QA.
- Skeptic / risk reviewer: claim safety, compliance guardrails, and overreach checks.

## Definition Of Done

- Round 1 proposals from at least three agent perspectives are recorded.
- Round 2 critiques/revisions are recorded.
- A decision brief exists and is usable without reading raw deliberation notes.
- Ticket creation is explicitly gated on Miguel approval.
