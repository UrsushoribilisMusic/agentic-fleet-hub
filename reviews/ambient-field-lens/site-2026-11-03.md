# Ambient Field Lens — submission-site review

Reviewer: Codi. Date: 2026-11-03. Target: https://afl.flotilla.cc/

## Assessment

8/10 as a submission companion. The site communicates the work more faithfully than the v12 video: it explains the failed readout transfer, shows immutable before/after retrieval evidence, distinguishes existing infrastructure from hackathon work, and discloses the phone-report transport failure. Keep those disclosures. The main weaknesses are an overconfident opening and the distance readers must scroll to reach the strongest proof.

This is an editorial/technical assessment, not a predicted judging result. I did not rerun the experiments or execute the live assist pipeline.

## Fix before submission

1. **Align the opening with the evidence.** “Measures whether your documentation covers the question, refuses when it does not” promises reliable coverage classification. The page itself reports two of three near-misses on the accepted side and states that distance is not relevance. The mobile loop's “Same question — now grounded” makes the same unsupported leap. Use a narrower, stronger demonstrated claim: “A field assistant that turns missing documentation into reviewed knowledge. It checks retrieved evidence, flags possible gaps, and lets an expert improve the manual. Ask again and see exactly which new passage it cites.” Explain that the distance check is provisional; the card remains verify-only. Apply the wording to the meta description and poster alt text too.

2. **Move the receipt directly below the film.** On desktop the evidence starts at y=16,870 (900px viewport); on phone at y=16,056 (844px viewport), about 19 screen heights down in both cases. Hero anchor links help, but the default reading path contains 16 full-height animation steps before the proof. Show the exact question, before/after passage excerpts, changed chunk IDs and unchanged refusal control early. Link directly to the relevant repository receipts/full texts, not just the repository root. Keep the animated explanation after that or make it a compact optional tour.

3. **Correct the geometry claim.** The disclosure says “SAM 3.1 scene gating with millimetre geometry.” The local current main checkout, eaab520, backend/live_backend.py explicitly says measured geometry is unaccepted and footprint clearance stays unmeasured. Separate implemented experimental geometry from demonstrated live behavior: “SAM paper-mask candidates; experimental measured geometry, not accepted for live clearance.” Do not imply live accepted millimetre containment.

4. **Correct the deployment diagram/copy.** The system section puts segmentation and Glimmer GPU work under the same Modal-shaped story. Explicitly label SAM 3.1 as a Meta API call and Glimmer as Modal GPU work; retrieval is CPU. This both avoids an infrastructure misstatement and makes actual Meta API use clearer to judges.

## Presentation refinements

- The chart increases distance downward (0.30 at top, 0.90 at bottom). “Two of the three fall below the gate” refers to numerical values, but the highlighted dots are visibly above the red line. Use an ordinary increasing-upward axis, or say “two near-misses are on the accepted side” and label both regions. Do not let a judge mentally invert the plot.
- Replace “Nothing deploys unsigned” with “Nothing deploys without human approval” unless cryptographic signing is actually implemented.
- Start with the technician's problem and the documentation-repair outcome. “This is not a RAG chatbot” spends the first sentence arguing a category instead of demonstrating differentiation. One sentence connecting missing instructions to delayed commissioning/repeat visits would strengthen the impact case; do not claim measured savings.
- On a 390px phone the evidence table is intentionally horizontally scrollable, but source identifiers are cut off without an obvious scroll cue. Prefer stacked Before/After cards, or add a visible horizontal-scroll affordance and direct receipt links.
- Mobile has dedicated vertical diagrams, which is good. At sampled intermediate steps the bottom caption covers some downstream diagram nodes. Reserve caption space so the full diagram remains inspectable. Tablet diagrams are small relative to the large blank stage, though readable; a compact tablet layout would help.
- The opening badges emphasize Glimmer, while SAM is missing from the main system diagram. Give each actual Meta contribution its own labelled box; explicitly distinguish demonstrated phone capture from a glasses-compatible interface. Do not imply a glasses capture was demonstrated unless it was.

## Keep

- Film near the top; working click-to-play embed; direct evidence/limits/code anchors.
- Exact before/after distances (0.8199 → 0.3997), collection counts (38 → 39), changed citation IDs, preserved original corpus and rollback.
- Body-only and neutral-heading controls: much more persuasive than distance movement alone.
- Explicit negative readout transfer and no gating based on that score. The small pilot's limitations are appropriately stated.
- Honest limitations: always verify, mask candidates only, no capture-time proof, distance not relevance, manual service-identity fallback after phone reporting failed.
- Clear separation of pre-existing arm/wiki infrastructure from hackathon-built work.

## Judging implications

- **Impact (30%)**: make the technician/equipment-manufacturer benefit explicit near the top. The reusable documentation loop is the strongest business story.
- **Meta tooling (25%)**: show SAM/Meta API, open-weight Glimmer introspection, and DAT's actual role separately. A negative experiment can demonstrate depth, but should not be sold as a working grounding gate.
- **Technical depth (20%)**: receipts, immutable corpus pins, controls and honest negative results are strong. Direct artifact links and corrected geometry wording would make them easier to verify.
- **Demo (25%)**: the film is accessible and plays. Make the visible payoff immediately available on the page and reduce the scroll burden. The website cannot substitute for what judges must understand from the film itself.
